# Use Wispr Flow with ChatGPT and Claude

<DisclosureNotice />

<HowToSchema name="Dictate in ChatGPT and Claude with Wispr Flow" appName="ChatGPT and Claude" />

**Because Wispr Flow types wherever your cursor is, it works in the ChatGPT and Claude text boxes with no integration or plugin. Put your cursor in the prompt field, hold the hotkey, and speak your prompt. This is one of the best uses for voice input, since good prompts are long and you think faster out loud than you type.**

## Why dictation and AI chat go well together

- **Longer, richer prompts.** People type short, lazy prompts. Speaking a paragraph of context is effortless, and better prompts get better answers.
- **Fewer round trips.** You can talk through the full task once instead of drip-feeding details.
- **It cleans as you go.** Filler words and false starts are stripped, so your prompt reads clearly.

## How to set it up

There is nothing special to configure. Install Wispr Flow on your [Mac](/docs/use/mac/) or [Windows](/docs/use/windows/) machine, open ChatGPT or Claude in your browser or desktop app, click into the message box, then hold your dictation hotkey and speak. Release, review the text, and send.

## A practical habit

Dictate the *what* and the *context* by voice, then type the precise bits (exact names, code, numbers) with the keyboard. That split plays to each input method's strength and is faster than either alone.
