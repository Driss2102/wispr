# How to use Wispr Flow on Android

<DisclosureNotice />

<HowToSchema name="Dictate in Android with Wispr Flow" appName="Android" />

**On Android, Wispr Flow works as a keyboard and, notably, the free plan gives unlimited dictation (versus a weekly cap on desktop and iPhone). Install the app, enable the Wispr Flow keyboard, grant microphone access, and dictate in any app.**

## Install and set up (step by step)

1. Install Wispr Flow from Google Play and open it to sign in.
2. Go to **Settings > System > Languages & input > On-screen keyboard > Manage keyboards** and enable Wispr Flow.
3. Grant **microphone** permission when asked.
4. In any text field, switch to the Wispr Flow keyboard and start dictating.

## Why Android is the best deal on the free plan

As of 2026, Wispr Flow's free plan is **unlimited on Android**, while desktop is capped at 2,000 words a week and iPhone at 1,000. If most of your dictation happens on a phone, Android is the cheapest way to use Wispr Flow heavily without paying. We keep this in the [free plan guide](/docs/review/wispr-flow-free-plan/).

## Dictating on Android

Tap to start, speak naturally with pauses, and Wispr Flow inserts cleaned-up, punctuated text. It works across messaging, email, notes, and browser fields.
