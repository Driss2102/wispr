# Use Wispr Flow in Obsidian

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow types into Obsidian's editor like any app: place your cursor in a note, hold your hotkey, and speak. It is a good fit for voice-first note taking, where you want to capture thinking quickly and tidy the markdown later.**

## Voice-first note taking

Obsidian rewards fast capture and later linking. Dictating the raw note (an idea, a meeting recap, a reading summary) gets it into your vault quickly, and Wispr Flow's cleanup means you are not editing a messy transcript. You then add links, tags, and headings by keyboard.

## How to use it

Install Wispr Flow on [Mac](/docs/use/mac/) or [Windows](/docs/use/windows/), open Obsidian, click into a note, hold the hotkey, and dictate. Markdown syntax (##, links, tags) is still typed; dictation is for the prose.

## Tips

- Keep a daily note open and dictate into it throughout the day.
- Add recurring names and project terms to your [custom dictionary](/docs/guides/custom-dictionary/).
- Say punctuation only when you need a specific structure; otherwise let the auto-cleanup handle it.
