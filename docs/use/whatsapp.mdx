# Use Wispr Flow in WhatsApp

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow types into WhatsApp like any app: on your phone, switch to the Wispr Flow keyboard, tap to dictate, and it inserts clean text into the message box. This is different from a WhatsApp voice note: instead of sending audio the other person has to listen to, you send readable text, faster to write than typing and easier for them to skim.**

## Text dictation vs a voice note

A WhatsApp voice message is easy for you but slow for the reader, who has to listen and cannot skim or search it. Dictating with Wispr Flow gives you the speed of speaking with the courtesy of text: the recipient gets a clean, skimmable message. For most chats, that is the better trade.

## How to use it

On [iPhone](/docs/use/iphone/) or [Android](/docs/use/android/), enable the Wispr Flow keyboard, open a WhatsApp chat, switch to the keyboard, and dictate. On desktop, use WhatsApp Web with Wispr Flow installed on your [Mac](/docs/use/mac/) or [Windows](/docs/use/windows/) machine.

## A note on etiquette and privacy

Dictating out loud in public is a courtesy question, and dictation is processed in the cloud by default. For quick personal messages that is fine; for sensitive content, keep the usual caution.
