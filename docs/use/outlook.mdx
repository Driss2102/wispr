# Use Wispr Flow in Outlook

<DisclosureNotice />

<HowToSchema name="Dictate in Outlook with Wispr Flow" appName="Outlook" />

**Wispr Flow types into Outlook like any app: open a new email, click into the body, hold your hotkey, and speak. Because email is mostly prose, Outlook is one of the best places to use dictation. Wispr Flow adds punctuation, removes filler, and can match a professional tone, so dictated email reads like written email.**

## Why Outlook is a great fit

High-volume inboxes are exactly where dictation saves time. Speaking replies is faster than typing them, and the automatic cleanup means you are not sending a messy transcript. It complements Outlook's own tools rather than replacing them.

## How to use it

Install Wispr Flow on [Mac](/docs/use/mac/) or [Windows](/docs/use/windows/), open Outlook (desktop or web), click into the subject or body, hold the hotkey, and dictate. Use the mobile keyboard for the Outlook app on your phone.

## A fast inbox routine

1. Triage first, deciding what needs a reply.
2. Dictate each reply in one pass.
3. Fix names, figures, and links by keyboard, then send.

More on this in our post on [clearing your inbox by dictating](/blog/dictate-emails-faster/).
