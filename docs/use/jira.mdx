# Use Wispr Flow in Jira

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow types into Jira like any web app: click into a ticket description or comment, hold your hotkey, and speak. It is a real time-saver for the prose parts of project work, ticket descriptions, acceptance criteria, comments, and updates, which are the parts people most often skip or rush. Structured fields (labels, assignees, statuses) are still faster with the keyboard and mouse.**

## Where it helps in Jira

- **Ticket descriptions.** Talk out the context and requirements instead of typing a terse title and nothing else.
- **Comments and updates.** Quick status notes and replies, dictated in seconds.
- **Acceptance criteria and steps to reproduce.** Prose that benefits from being spoken and cleaned up.

## Where the keyboard wins

Selecting fields, setting labels and assignees, linking issues, and navigating boards are point-and-click actions, not dictation. Use voice for the writing, the interface for the structure.

## How to use it

Install Wispr Flow on [Mac](/docs/use/mac/) or [Windows](/docs/use/windows/), open Jira in your browser, click into a description or comment, hold your hotkey, and dictate. Add your team, product, and component names to a [custom dictionary](/docs/guides/custom-dictionary/) so they transcribe correctly.
