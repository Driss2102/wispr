# Use Wispr Flow in Discord

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow types into Discord like any app: click the message box, hold your hotkey, and speak. It is handy for busy servers and DMs where you send lots of short messages, and for moments when your hands are busy (gaming, for example) but you still want to post text rather than jump into voice chat.**

## Why it fits Discord

Discord is high-volume, casual chat. Dictation is quick for the short, frequent messages that fill a server, and Wispr Flow keeps them readable with automatic punctuation and a casual tone. It is text, not voice chat, so it works in text channels and threads where a voice call would not fit.

## How to use it

Install Wispr Flow on [Mac](/docs/use/mac/) or [Windows](/docs/use/windows/), open the Discord app or web, click into a channel or DM, hold your hotkey, and dictate. On mobile, use the Wispr Flow keyboard in the Discord app.

## Text dictation vs Discord voice chat

Discord voice channels are for talking live. Wispr Flow is for posting text quickly by speaking. Use voice chat to hang out; use dictation to fire off messages in text channels without typing every word.
