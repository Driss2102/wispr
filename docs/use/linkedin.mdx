# Use Wispr Flow for LinkedIn

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow types into LinkedIn like any web app: click the post box, comment field, or message, hold your hotkey, and speak. It suits LinkedIn well because posts and DMs are prose, and consistent posting is mostly a volume problem. Dictating a draft and editing it is faster than typing from scratch, and the tone modes help keep it professional.**

## Why it fits LinkedIn

Regular posting is hard because writing is slow, not because ideas are scarce. Talking out a post draft removes the blank-page friction; you then trim and sharpen it. The same applies to thoughtful comments and DMs, where speaking keeps you natural.

## Keep it professional

Dictated LinkedIn content can drift too casual. Use a more formal tone mode, and always edit: cut the filler, tighten the hook, and check names and links by keyboard. Dictation gets the draft out; the edit makes it publishable.

## How to use it

Install Wispr Flow on [Mac](/docs/use/mac/) or [Windows](/docs/use/windows/), open LinkedIn in your browser, click into the post or message box, hold your hotkey, and dictate. On mobile, use the Wispr Flow keyboard in the LinkedIn app.
