# Use Wispr Flow in Telegram

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow types into Telegram like any app: click the message box, hold your hotkey (desktop) or use the Wispr Flow keyboard (mobile), and speak. It gives you the speed of a voice note with the readability of text, so the person on the other end can skim and search your message instead of listening to audio.**

## Text beats a voice note for the reader

Telegram voice messages are quick to send but slow to consume. Dictating with Wispr Flow keeps the speed for you while sending clean, skimmable text, better for group chats and anyone who prefers reading.

## How to use it

On desktop, install Wispr Flow on your [Mac](/docs/use/mac/) or [Windows](/docs/use/windows/) machine, open Telegram, click into a chat, hold your hotkey, and dictate. On mobile, use the Wispr Flow keyboard in the Telegram app on [iPhone](/docs/use/iphone/) or [Android](/docs/use/android/).
