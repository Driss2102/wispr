# Use Wispr Flow in Notion

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow types into Notion like any editor: click into a page or block, hold your hotkey, and speak. It is excellent for quick capture (notes, meeting recaps, brain dumps) and less suited to heavily formatted docs, where you will still reach for the keyboard and slash commands.**

## Best for quick capture

The fastest way to fill a blank Notion page is to talk. Dictate the raw content, let Wispr Flow clean it up, then structure it with Notion's blocks afterward. This beats typing and formatting at the same time, which slows both down.

## How to use it

Install Wispr Flow on [Mac](/docs/use/mac/) or [Windows](/docs/use/windows/), open Notion, click into a block, hold the hotkey, and dictate. Use the mobile keyboard for the Notion app on [iPhone](/docs/use/iphone/) or [Android](/docs/use/android/).

## Where it is weaker

Notion's power is structure: toggles, tables, callouts, databases. Dictation does not place those for you, so complex layouts are still a keyboard job. Think of Wispr Flow as the fast way to get words in, not to build the page.
