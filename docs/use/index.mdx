# Use Wispr Flow on your device or app

<DisclosureNotice />

## How setup works, in short

1. Download Wispr Flow for your device from the official site and install it.
2. Grant microphone and accessibility permissions (this is what lets it type into other apps).
3. Learn your dictation hotkey, put your cursor in any text field, hold it, and speak.
