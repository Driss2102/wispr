# Best Wispr Flow alternatives (2026)

<DisclosureNotice />

**The best Wispr Flow alternative depends on what you value. Superwhisper is the closest direct rival and the pick if you want on-device model options. Typeless offers a larger free word cap. Apple Dictation and Windows Voice Typing are free built-ins that are enough for light use. Otter is for meetings, not system-wide dictation. Dragon is the heavy-duty professional option. OpenAI Whisper is a model, not an app.**

## How to choose

1. **Just want it to work everywhere with minimal fuss?** Wispr Flow or Superwhisper.
2. **On a tight or zero budget?** Start with your OS built-in (Apple Dictation or Windows Voice Typing), or Wispr Flow's free plan (unlimited on Android).
3. **Need offline/on-device processing?** Superwhisper or a Whisper-based tool.
4. **Recording and summarizing meetings?** Otter, not a dictation app.
