# Wispr Flow vs Typeless

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow and Typeless are close rivals in AI dictation. The headline difference is the free tier: Typeless advertises a larger free weekly word allowance than Wispr Flow. Wispr Flow counters with broader platform support (including Android, unlimited on its free plan) and very consistent cleanup. If the free word cap is your deciding factor, Typeless is worth a look; otherwise Wispr Flow is the more complete app.**

This is an honest, two-sided comparison. If Wispr Flow is your choice, see our [review](/docs/review/).

## Where Wispr Flow wins

- **Platform coverage.** Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android, with unlimited free dictation on Android.
- **Consistent, lighter-touch cleanup** that stays close to what you said.
- **Mobile apps** on both major phone platforms.

## Where Typeless wins

- **Bigger free allowance** for desktop users who dictate a lot without paying.
- **More aggressive auto-editing**, which some writers prefer for polished output.

## Which should you pick?

If you dictate heavily on desktop and the free cap decides it, try Typeless first. If you want one app that follows you to your phone and edits with a lighter touch, Wispr Flow fits more workflows. Both have free tiers, so test on real writing.
