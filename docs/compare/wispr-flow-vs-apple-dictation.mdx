# Wispr Flow vs Apple Dictation

<DisclosureNotice />

**Apple Dictation is free, built into every Mac and iPhone, and fine for short, clear dictation. Wispr Flow is a paid app that handles rambling speech far better, cleans up filler words, adds punctuation intelligently, and matches tone. If you dictate occasionally, Apple Dictation is enough. If you dictate daily and want output you can send without editing, Wispr Flow earns its price.**

This is an honest, two-sided comparison. If Wispr Flow wins for you, read the [review](/docs/review/).

## When Apple Dictation is enough

- You dictate short messages occasionally.
- You are on Apple devices only and want zero cost.
- You do not mind saying "comma" and "new paragraph" or lightly editing after.

## When Wispr Flow is worth paying for

- You dictate daily across many apps and want clean output with no manual punctuation.
- You ramble, pause, and self-correct, and want the app to sort it out.
- You work across Windows or Android too, not just Apple.

## Our take

Do not pay if built-in dictation already covers your needs; that is the honest answer. But if you have tried Apple Dictation and found yourself editing every result, Wispr Flow's cleanup is exactly the gap it fills.
