# Wispr Flow vs Speechify

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow and Speechify overlap on dictation but are built for different jobs. Wispr Flow is a specialized AI dictation app: you speak, it types clean text into any app. Speechify is a broad voice suite whose main strength is text-to-speech, reading articles and documents aloud, with voice typing added alongside. Choose Wispr Flow if your goal is fast, hands-off dictation; choose Speechify if you also want your content read to you.**

This is an honest, two-sided comparison. If Wispr Flow is your pick, read our [full review](/docs/review/).

## Where Wispr Flow wins

- **Specialized dictation.** Dictation is the entire product, not a side feature, so the auto-cleanup and tone matching are a core focus. See our [accuracy](/docs/review/wispr-flow-accuracy/) page.
- **More languages.** Wispr Flow supports 100+ [languages](/docs/review/wispr-flow-languages/) versus 60+ for Speechify.
- **Mobile dictation.** Apps on iPhone and Android, with unlimited free dictation on Android. Start on the [free plan](/docs/review/wispr-flow-free-plan/).

## Where Speechify wins

- **Reads text aloud.** This is the big one: Speechify's main job is text-to-speech, turning articles, PDFs, and docs into audio. Wispr Flow does not do this at all.
- **All-in-one voice.** Reading, dictation, and an AI assistant live in one app, which suits people who want both directions of voice in a single tool.
- **Reach.** A very large user base and wide platform coverage, including the browser.

## Which should you pick?

Decide by what you actually need. If you want to turn speech into text quickly and cleanly, Wispr Flow is the specialist and the better dictation experience. If your main need is having content read aloud to you, or you want reading and dictation bundled together, Speechify's suite makes more sense. They are not really the same category, and for pure dictation Wispr Flow is the focused pick.
