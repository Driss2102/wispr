## Where Wispr Flow wins

- **Hands-off polish.** Its cleanup and tone matching are consistently strong, so output needs less editing.
- **Mobile.** Apps on both iPhone and Android, with unlimited free dictation on Android.
- **Simplicity.** Less to configure; it just works across apps.

## Where Superwhisper wins

- **On-device / offline options.** If you do not want speech leaving your machine, Superwhisper's local model options are the main draw.
- **Model choice.** More control for people who like to tune which model runs.

## Which should you pick?

If privacy or offline use is a hard requirement, Superwhisper is the safer default. For most people who just want fast, clean dictation everywhere, including on a phone, Wispr Flow is the smoother experience. Try both free tiers on your real work before deciding.
