# Wispr Flow vs Otter.ai

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow and Otter.ai are often compared but solve different problems. Wispr Flow is system-wide dictation: you speak and it types into any app. Otter.ai is a meeting assistant: it records calls, transcribes speakers, and produces summaries. If you want to replace your keyboard, choose Wispr Flow. If you want notes from meetings, choose Otter.**

This is an honest, two-sided comparison. If Wispr Flow suits you, read the [review](/docs/review/).

## Choose Wispr Flow if...

You want to write emails, messages, docs, and prompts faster by speaking. It is a keyboard replacement, not a meeting recorder.

## Choose Otter if...

Your real need is transcripts and summaries of meetings, with speaker labels and searchable notes. Otter is built for that; Wispr Flow is not.

## Can you use both?

Yes, and many people do: Wispr Flow for day-to-day writing, Otter for meetings. They do not overlap much.
