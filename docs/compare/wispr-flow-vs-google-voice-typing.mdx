# Wispr Flow vs Google Voice Typing

<DisclosureNotice />

**Google Voice Typing (in Google Docs and via Gboard on Android) is free and good for basic dictation, but it lives mainly inside Google's apps and does lighter cleanup. Wispr Flow works system-wide across any app, cleans up filler and punctuation more thoroughly, and runs on desktop and mobile. For Google Docs-only, casual use, Google is fine. For dictating everywhere with polished output, Wispr Flow is stronger.**

This is an honest, two-sided comparison. If Wispr Flow suits you, read the [review](/docs/review/).

## Choose Google Voice Typing if...

You work mostly in Google Docs or dictate casually on Android with Gboard, and you want a free option that is already there.

## Choose Wispr Flow if...

You want to dictate in any app (email clients, Slack, editors, AI tools), on desktop and mobile, with cleaner output that needs less editing.
