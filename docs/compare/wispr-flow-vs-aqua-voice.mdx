# Wispr Flow vs Aqua Voice

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow and Aqua Voice are both AI dictation apps, but they optimize for different things. Aqua Voice runs its own proprietary model (Avalon) and leans hard on published accuracy benchmarks, positioning itself for developers and technical users, at a lower monthly price. Wispr Flow leans on breadth: more platforms (including Android), 100+ languages, and team governance. If benchmarked accuracy on a Mac is your priority, look at Aqua; if you want reach and mobile, Wispr Flow fits more setups.**

This is an honest, two-sided comparison. If Wispr Flow is your pick, read the [review](/docs/review/).

## Where Wispr Flow wins

- **Platform reach.** It runs on Android; Aqua does not.
- **Languages.** 100+ with auto-detection versus Aqua's roughly 49.
- **Teams.** SSO, HIPAA, and admin controls on higher plans.

## Where Aqua Voice wins

- **Published accuracy.** Aqua promotes benchmark results for its Avalon model and targets technical users; Wispr Flow does not publish benchmarks.
- **Price.** Aqua's monthly plan is cheaper than Wispr Flow's.

## Which should you pick?

If you want maximum measured accuracy on a Mac or Windows machine for technical dictation, and Android and wide language support do not matter to you, Aqua Voice is worth testing. If you want one app across all your devices (including your Android phone), many languages, and team features, Wispr Flow is the more flexible choice. Both have free entry points, so test on your own voice.
