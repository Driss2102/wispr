# Wispr Flow vs Willow Voice

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow and Willow Voice are unusually similar: both are cross-platform AI dictation apps with automatic cleanup, tone matching, 100+ languages, and the same free allowance of 2,000 words per week. The differences are at the margins, context handling, latency claims, and team pricing, so the honest advice is to try both free tiers and pick the one whose accuracy and feel you prefer on your own voice.**

This is an honest, two-sided comparison. If Wispr Flow is your pick, read the [review](/docs/review/).

## The honest truth: they are close

These two overlap more than almost any other pair in this space. Both remove filler, add punctuation, match tone to the app, support 100+ languages, and work across your apps. If a friend used one and liked it, that is a perfectly good reason to pick it.

## Small tie-breakers

- **Android free:** Wispr Flow's free plan is unlimited on Android, a real edge for heavy phone users.
- **Context handling:** Willow markets context-aware transcription for jargon and proper nouns; Wispr Flow uses a [custom dictionary](/docs/guides/custom-dictionary/) for the same goal.
- **Company backing:** Wispr Flow is a well-funded company valued at around $2 billion as of 2026 (see [the company](/docs/wispr-flow-company/)), which some buyers weigh for longevity.

## Which should you pick?

Try both free tiers on a week of real writing. Judge accuracy on your voice, how the cleanup feels, and whether Android-unlimited matters to you. There is no wrong answer here; they are genuinely close.
