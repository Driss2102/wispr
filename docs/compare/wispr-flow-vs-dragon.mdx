# Wispr Flow vs Dragon

<DisclosureNotice />

**Dragon is the long-standing professional dictation tool, strong on high-accuracy, high-volume dictation and specialized vocabularies (for example medical and legal), with a higher price and a steeper setup. Wispr Flow is modern, affordable AI dictation that works across everyday apps with almost no configuration. For professional, structured dictation Dragon still leads; for general daily writing Wispr Flow is simpler and cheaper.**

This is an honest, two-sided comparison. If Wispr Flow fits you, read the [review](/docs/review/).

## Choose Wispr Flow if...

You want fast, clean dictation across email, chat, docs, and AI tools without a big setup or price. It fits general knowledge work and cross-platform use.

## Choose Dragon if...

You do high-volume, structured dictation in a specialized field and need deep custom vocabularies and command control. Dragon's professional heritage still shows here.
