# Wispr Flow vs VoiceInk

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow and VoiceInk solve the same problem in opposite ways. Wispr Flow is a polished, cloud-based dictation app that works across Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android on a subscription. VoiceInk is an open-source, Mac-only app that runs entirely on your device for a one-time purchase. Choose Wispr Flow for hands-off polish and cross-platform reach; choose VoiceInk for local privacy, offline use, and no subscription.**

This is an honest, two-sided comparison. If Wispr Flow is your pick, read our [full review](/docs/review/).

## Where Wispr Flow wins

- **Cross-platform and mobile.** VoiceInk is Mac-only. Wispr Flow follows you to Windows, iPhone, and Android, with unlimited free dictation on Android.
- **Hands-off polish.** Its automatic cleanup and tone matching are consistently strong, so output needs less editing.
- **Zero setup.** No models to install or configure; you sign in and dictate. Start on the [free plan](/docs/review/wispr-flow-free-plan/).

## Where VoiceInk wins

- **Privacy and offline use.** VoiceInk runs on-device with whisper.cpp, so audio never leaves your Mac and it works with no connection. Wispr Flow is [cloud by default](/blog/cloud-vs-on-device-dictation/).
- **One-time purchase.** VoiceInk is a single payment with no subscription, which some people strongly prefer. See [is there a Wispr Flow lifetime deal](/blog/wispr-flow-lifetime-deal/).
- **Open source.** It is GPLv3, so you can inspect and trust the code.

## Which should you pick?

If you are on a Mac, care about privacy or offline use, and dislike subscriptions, VoiceInk is a genuinely good fit. If you work across more than one device, want dictation on your phone, or prefer the most hands-off cleanup with no setup, Wispr Flow is the smoother experience. Both are worth trying on your real work before deciding.
