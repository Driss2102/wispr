# Wispr Flow languages

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow supports more than 100 languages with automatic language detection, so most major languages, including Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Hindi, Arabic, Mandarin, and many more, are covered, and you can switch between them without changing a setting. Accuracy is strongest on clear speech in widely spoken languages, and can vary with strong accents or less-common languages.**

## How multilingual dictation works

You do not pick a language before you speak. Wispr Flow detects the language automatically, so you can dictate one message in English and the next in Spanish without touching a menu. For bilingual users who switch languages constantly, this is the standout feature.

## Switching and mixing languages

Because detection is automatic, moving between languages is smooth in practice: dictate in whichever language comes naturally. If you frequently mix languages in one sentence, results vary, so test your own pattern on the free plan.

## Accents and less-common languages

- **Widely spoken languages, clear speech:** strong accuracy.
- **Strong or less-common accents:** more errors, as with any dictation tool. A [custom dictionary](/docs/guides/custom-dictionary/) helps with names and terms.
- **Rare languages:** supported within the 100+, but accuracy may be lower than for major languages.

This makes Wispr Flow a strong pick for [non-native English speakers](/docs/for/non-native-english-speakers/), who can write in their first language or in English.

## How to check your language

The simplest check is to try it: install the [free plan](/docs/review/wispr-flow-free-plan/) and dictate a few sentences in your language. For the current official list, see Wispr Flow's own site, since the supported set grows over time.
