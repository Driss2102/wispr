# Is Wispr Flow free?

<DisclosureNotice />

**Yes, Wispr Flow has a real, permanent free plan, not a time-limited trial (if you were looking for a [free trial](/docs/review/wispr-flow-free-trial/), the free plan is better). As of 2026 it gives you 2,000 words per week on desktop, 1,000 per week on iPhone, and, notably, unlimited words on Android. No credit card is required. For most people the free plan is enough to decide whether dictation fits their workflow, and Android users can run it heavily for free.**

## What the free plan includes

- Dictation in 100+ languages across Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android.
- Automatic cleanup: filler removal, punctuation, tone.
- Personal dictionary and snippets.
- Limited Notetaker access.

The main limit is the **weekly word cap**, not a feature lockout. You get the core experience; you just cannot dictate unlimited amounts on desktop or iPhone.

## The word caps, by device (2026)

- **Desktop (Mac and Windows):** about 2,000 words per week.
- **iPhone:** about 1,000 words per week.
- **Android:** unlimited on the free plan.

That Android detail is unusual and genuinely useful: if most of your dictation is on a phone, Android lets you use Wispr Flow heavily without paying. See the [Android setup guide](/docs/use/android/).

## Free plan vs free trial: which is it?

People often search for a "Wispr Flow free trial," expecting a 7 or 14 day countdown. That framing does not really apply here, because the free plan is permanent, not a trial you lose access to. You do not need to remember to cancel anything, and you are not on a clock. If you specifically want to test the paid features, see [Wispr Flow free trial](/docs/review/wispr-flow-free-trial/); for most people the free plan is the better starting point.

## How to get the most out of the free plan

- **Dictate your highest-value prose first.** Spend your weekly words where speed helps most: email, drafts, and AI prompts, not one-word edits.
- **Set up a custom dictionary early.** It stops you wasting words re-correcting names and jargon. See the [custom dictionary guide](/docs/guides/custom-dictionary/).
- **Lean on Android for heavy days.** Since Android is unlimited, dictate longer pieces there and sync the text back.

## Is the free plan enough for you?

- **Light or occasional dictation:** yes, easily.
- **Mostly on Android:** yes, it is unlimited there.
- **Heavy daily dictation on desktop:** you will hit the cap; look at [Pro](/docs/review/wispr-flow-pro/) and whether it is [worth it](/docs/review/is-wispr-flow-worth-it/).

## The honest downside

2,000 words a week is not a lot if dictation becomes your main input method; that is a few long emails and some notes. The free plan is a genuine tool and a fair way to decide, but heavy desktop users are clearly nudged toward Pro. That is a reasonable model, but worth knowing going in. For the full plan breakdown, see the [pricing page](/docs/review/wispr-flow-pricing/).
