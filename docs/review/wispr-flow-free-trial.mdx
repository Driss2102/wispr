# Wispr Flow free trial

<DisclosureNotice />

**If you are searching for a Wispr Flow "free trial", here is the honest answer: what exists is better than a trial. Wispr Flow has a permanent free plan, not a 7-day countdown. You can use it indefinitely, with a weekly word cap on desktop and iPhone (2,000 and 1,000 words per week) and unlimited dictation on Android, with no credit card. So there is nothing to expire and nothing to cancel if you never upgrade.**

## Free plan vs a traditional trial

A traditional free trial gives you full features for a short time, then charges you. Wispr Flow's model is different: the [free plan](/docs/review/wispr-flow-free-plan/) is permanent but limited by a weekly word count rather than by time. That is friendlier for testing, because you can keep using it at your own pace without a ticking clock or a card on file.

## How to "trial" the Pro features

- **Use the free plan first** to judge accuracy, cleanup, and fit on your real work.
- **Watch whether you hit the weekly cap.** If you do, that is your signal Pro is worth it.
- **On Android, you may never need Pro,** since the free plan is unlimited there.
- If you do subscribe and change your mind, [cancelling](/docs/guides/how-to-cancel-wispr-flow/) simply drops you back to the free plan.

## The bottom line

Do not wait for a trial that does not exist. Download Wispr Flow, use the free plan for a week of real writing, and let hitting (or not hitting) the word cap tell you whether to pay.
