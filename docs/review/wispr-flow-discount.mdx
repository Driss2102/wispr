# Wispr Flow discount and promo codes

<DisclosureNotice />

**There is no permanent public promo code for Wispr Flow, and any site claiming a secret "90% off" code is almost certainly fake. The real ways to pay less are honest and reliable: use the free plan, switch to annual billing (about 20% cheaper per month than monthly), and use the referral program to earn free Pro time. Occasional seasonal offers may appear on Wispr Flow's own site.**

## The honest ways to pay less

### 1. The free plan
The simplest discount is not paying at all until you need to. The [free plan](/docs/review/wispr-flow-free-plan/) has a weekly word cap on desktop and iPhone and is unlimited on Android. Use it to decide before spending anything.

### 2. Annual billing
Paying yearly instead of monthly is the biggest built-in saving: annual Pro is roughly 20 percent cheaper per month than monthly. If you already know dictation fits your workflow, this is the real discount. Current figures are on the [pricing page](/docs/review/wispr-flow-pricing/).

### 3. The referral program
As of 2026, Wispr Flow has a "refer a friend" program: your friend gets a 30-day Pro trial, and once they dictate a set amount you earn a month of Pro. Referring a few people can stack up free Pro time. Check the current terms in the app, since referral rewards change.

### 4. Watch for official seasonal offers
Any genuine discount comes from Wispr Flow itself (for example a launch or holiday offer). Look on their [official site](https://wisprflow.ai/), not on coupon-aggregator sites.

## Avoid fake coupon sites

Coupon-aggregator pages that promise huge Wispr Flow discounts usually list expired or invented codes to farm clicks. If a code is not on Wispr Flow's own site or in the app, treat it as fake. We would rather tell you the free plan and annual billing are your real levers than send you chasing dead codes.
