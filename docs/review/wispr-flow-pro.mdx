# Is Wispr Flow Pro worth it?

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow Pro is worth it for one clear reason: you regularly hit the free plan's weekly word cap. Pro removes the cap for unlimited dictation and adds longer Notetaker history and priority support. If you dictate daily across email, chat, and docs, it pays for itself in saved time. If you dictate occasionally, or mostly on Android (where free is unlimited), stay on Free.**

## What Pro adds over Free

- **Unlimited dictation** (the main reason to upgrade).
- **Longer Notetaker history.**
- **Priority support** and **early access** to new features.

The core dictation quality is the same on Free and Pro; you are paying to remove the weekly cap, not to unlock a better engine.

## The simple test

Ask yourself: *do I hit the free weekly cap?*

- **Yes, most weeks:** Pro is worth it. The friction of rationing words costs more than the plan.
- **No:** stay on Free. There is no reason to pay for a cap you never reach.
- **I dictate mostly on my phone:** if that phone is Android, Free is unlimited, so you may not need Pro at all.

## Monthly or annual?

Annual is cheaper per month. If you already know dictation is part of your workflow, annual is the better value. If you are still building the habit, start monthly. Current figures are on the [pricing page](/docs/review/wispr-flow-pricing/).
