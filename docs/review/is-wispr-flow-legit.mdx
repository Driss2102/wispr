# Is Wispr Flow legit?

<DisclosureNotice />

**Yes, Wispr Flow is legit. It is the product of Wispr AI, Inc., a real San Francisco company founded in 2021 that raised a $280 million Series B led by Menlo Ventures at a $2 billion valuation in August 2026 (per TechCrunch and Fortune). It is not a scam. The reasonable things to weigh are not legitimacy but the practical ones: it processes speech in the cloud by default, and it needs deep permissions to type into other apps.**

## Why it is trustworthy

- **A real, funded company.** Wispr AI is venture-backed by well-known investors and valued at around $2 billion as of 2026. See [the company](/docs/wispr-flow-company/).
- **A public product** with a website, app-store listings, a help center, and press coverage.
- **Widely used** across the dictation market, not a fly-by-night app.

## What to actually check (not legitimacy, but fit)

- **Cloud processing.** By default your speech is processed in the cloud. For most writing that is fine; for sensitive content, read [is Wispr Flow safe](/docs/review/is-wispr-flow-safe/).
- **Permissions.** It needs microphone access and, on Mac, Accessibility permission so it can type into other apps. That is normal for a dictation tool, but it is a broad permission, so only install from official sources.
- **Subscription.** The free plan is genuine; paid plans auto-renew like any subscription. See [how to cancel](/docs/guides/how-to-cancel-wispr-flow/) if you want the exit before you commit.

## Install it safely

Download only from the [official site or the app stores](/docs/download-wispr-flow/), never a third-party mirror. Grant only the permissions it asks for. That is the whole safety checklist for a legitimate app like this.
