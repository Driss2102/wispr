# Who is Wispr Flow for?

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow pays off most for people who write a lot in short bursts across many apps, and for anyone whose hands need a break from the keyboard. Below are honest takes by use case, including where dictation is not the right tool. Pick the one that matches you.**

Not sure which group is you? Start with [who uses Wispr Flow](/docs/for/who-uses-wispr-flow/).

## By role

- [Wispr Flow for solopreneurs and founders](/docs/for/solopreneurs/): handle high writing volume solo.
- [Wispr Flow for developers](/docs/for/developers/): prompts, comments, and commits by voice.
- [Wispr Flow for writers](/docs/for/writers/): fast first drafts you edit later.
- [Wispr Flow for students](/docs/for/students/): notes, essays, and study material.
- [Wispr Flow for customer support](/docs/for/customer-support/): faster replies at volume.
- [Wispr Flow for sales teams](/docs/for/sales/): CRM notes, follow-ups, and outreach by voice.
- [Wispr Flow for consultants](/docs/for/consultants/): call notes, follow-ups, and deliverables.
- [Wispr Flow for lawyers](/docs/for/lawyers/): draft correspondence and notes, with the confidentiality caveats.
- [Wispr Flow for doctors](/docs/for/doctors/): admin and notes, plus the HIPAA reality.
- [Wispr Flow for teachers](/docs/for/teachers/): feedback, emails, and lesson plans.
- [Wispr Flow for podcasters and creators](/docs/for/podcasters/): show notes, scripts, and captions.

## By need

- [Wispr Flow for people with RSI](/docs/for/rsi/): type less, rest your wrists.
- [Wispr Flow for people with dyslexia](/docs/for/dyslexia/): remove the spelling friction from writing.
- [Wispr Flow for non-native English speakers](/docs/for/non-native-english-speakers/): speak fluently, write cleanly.
- [Wispr Flow for people with ADHD](/docs/for/adhd/): capture thoughts before they vanish.
- [Wispr Flow for executives](/docs/for/executives/): clear email in the gaps between meetings.

## The honest filter

Dictation is a strong fit when your work is mostly prose and you write across many apps. It is a weaker fit when your work is exact syntax, when you cannot talk out loud in your space, or when you only write occasionally. If any of those is you, read the specific page, it will say so.
