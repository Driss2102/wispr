# Wispr Flow for sales teams

<DisclosureNotice />

**Sales reps lose real selling time to admin: CRM notes, follow-up emails, and personalizing outreach. Wispr Flow lets reps dictate all of it, logging a call while it is fresh, sending a tailored follow-up in seconds, and personalizing messages at volume. Combined with snippets for the standard parts, it cuts the typing tax that keeps reps out of conversations.**

## Where it helps reps

- **CRM notes.** Dictate call notes straight into the CRM field right after a call, so nothing is lost. See [in your browser](/docs/use/browser/).
- **Follow-up emails.** Speak a tailored recap and next step in seconds. See [in Gmail](/docs/use/gmail/) / [Outlook](/docs/use/outlook/).
- **Personalized outreach.** Dictate the personal opener; drop in a [snippet](/docs/guides/wispr-flow-snippets/) for the pitch.
- **Chat and social selling.** Fast, clean messages in [Slack](/docs/use/slack/), [LinkedIn](/docs/use/linkedin/), and DMs.

## The snippets plus dictation combo

The highest-leverage setup for sales is snippets for the repeatable parts (value props, scheduling links, signoffs) plus dictation for the personal parts (why this prospect, what you discussed). That balance keeps outreach both fast and human.

## Teams and admin

Rolling this out to a team? The [Growth plan](/docs/review/wispr-flow-pricing/) adds shared dictionaries and snippets, admin controls, and usage reporting. Keep customer data within your CRM and security policies.
