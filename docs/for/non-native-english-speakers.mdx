# Wispr Flow for non-native English speakers

<DisclosureNotice />

**If you speak a language more fluently than you type it, Wispr Flow helps: you talk, and it produces clean, punctuated text. It supports 100+ languages with automatic detection, so you can dictate in your strongest language or in English, and the automatic cleanup smooths out the small errors that slow non-native typists down.**

## Why it helps

- **Speaking beats typing** when a keyboard layout or spelling is not your first instinct.
- **Automatic punctuation and cleanup** handle the mechanics many non-native writers find fiddly.
- **100+ languages with auto-detection** means you are not locked to English; switch naturally.

## A useful workflow

1. Dictate the message in whichever language comes easiest.
2. Let Wispr Flow clean the punctuation and filler.
3. Review for meaning; edit anything important by keyboard.

## Honest limits

Dictation transcribes what you say; it does not translate or rewrite your grammar into perfect native English on its own. Accents can also affect accuracy. It is a strong aid for fluency and speed, not a substitute for a proofreading pass on important text. For that, pair it with an AI assistant, which you can also [dictate prompts to](/docs/use/chatgpt/).
