# Wispr Flow for consultants

<DisclosureNotice />

**Consulting runs on writing: call notes, follow-up emails, proposals, and deliverable drafts. Wispr Flow lets you turn thinking and post-call recall into clean text quickly, so more of your billable time goes to the work and less to the keyboard. The caveat is client confidentiality, since dictation is cloud-based by default, keep sensitive client material within your and your client's data rules.**

## Where it helps consultants

- **Post-call notes.** Recap a client call by voice while it is fresh, into your notes app.
- **Follow-up emails.** Dictate the recap and next steps in a fraction of the time. See [in Gmail](/docs/use/gmail/) and [in Outlook](/docs/use/outlook/).
- **Proposal and deliverable drafts.** Get the narrative down fast, then edit precisely.
- **Multi-client dictionaries.** Add each client's names and terms to a [custom dictionary](/docs/guides/custom-dictionary/).

## A confidentiality-aware workflow

1. Use it freely for your own notes and internal drafting.
2. For sensitive client data, follow the client's and your firm's data policies, and review [is Wispr Flow safe](/docs/review/is-wispr-flow-safe/).
3. Keep exact figures and names checked by keyboard before anything goes to a client.
