# Wispr Flow for writers

<DisclosureNotice />

**For writers, Wispr Flow is a drafting tool, not an editing tool. Speaking a messy first draft out loud is faster than typing one and beats staring at a blank page. You then edit with the keyboard, where precision matters. Used this way, dictation raises your output without hurting quality.**

## Where it helps

- **First drafts.** Talk the idea through; get words on the page fast.
- **Idea capture.** Catch a thought before it disappears, in a note or doc.
- **Long-form volume.** If you produce a lot, drafting by voice compounds.

## Where it does not

Line editing, precise phrasing, and formatting are keyboard work. Dictation gets you a rough draft quickly; the craft still happens in the edit. Do not expect polished prose straight from your mouth.

## A simple workflow

1. Dictate the draft in [Notion](/docs/use/notion/), [Obsidian](/docs/use/obsidian/), or your editor without stopping to fix things.
2. Let Wispr Flow clean filler and punctuation.
3. Switch to the keyboard and edit properly.

Separating drafting from editing is the productivity trick; dictation just makes the drafting stage much faster.
