# Wispr Flow for teachers

<DisclosureNotice />

**Teaching comes with a mountain of writing: student feedback, parent emails, lesson plans, and reports. Wispr Flow lets you dictate that load instead of typing it, which is a real time-saver during grading season. The one thing to watch is student data: because dictation is cloud-based by default, keep sensitive or identifiable student information in line with your school's privacy rules.**

This is not legal or compliance advice. Follow your school's data policies.

## Where it helps teachers

- **Feedback and comments.** Speak feedback on work far faster than typing it.
- **Parent and admin emails.** High volume, mostly prose.
- **Lesson plans and materials.** Draft by voice, then structure by keyboard.
- **Reports.** Get the narrative parts down quickly, then edit.

## Grading-season workflow

1. Dictate feedback comment by comment; let the cleanup handle punctuation.
2. Keep phrasing consistent with [snippets](/docs/guides/wispr-flow-features/) for common comments.
3. Add student names and subject terms to a [custom dictionary](/docs/guides/custom-dictionary/) so they are spelled right.

## A note on student data

Dictation is processed in the cloud by default. Avoid dictating identifiable student information unless your school has approved the tool and, where relevant, a compliant plan. See [is Wispr Flow safe](/docs/review/is-wispr-flow-safe/).
