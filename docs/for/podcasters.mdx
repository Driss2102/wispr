# Wispr Flow for podcasters and creators

<DisclosureNotice />

**Podcasters and creators write far more than they expect: show notes, episode descriptions, scripts, newsletters, and social captions. Wispr Flow lets you dictate all of it, which suits people who are already comfortable talking. One thing to be clear on: Wispr Flow is live dictation, not a tool for transcribing your recorded episodes, that job belongs to a file-transcription tool.**

## Where it helps creators

- **Show notes and descriptions.** Talk them out right after recording, while it is fresh.
- **Scripts and outlines.** Draft by voice, then tighten by keyboard.
- **Social captions and newsletters.** High-volume writing that suits dictation.
- **Idea capture.** Grab an episode idea the moment it lands, in any notes app.

## What it does not do

Wispr Flow does not transcribe your recorded audio files into episode transcripts. For that you need a transcription tool built for audio files, such as a [Whisper-based](/docs/compare/wispr-flow-vs-openai-whisper/) app or [MacWhisper](/docs/compare/wispr-flow-vs-macwhisper/). Wispr Flow is for writing live, as you speak.

## A creator workflow

1. Right after recording, dictate the show notes and description.
2. Draft the social posts by voice in one pass.
3. Edit for hooks and formatting by keyboard.
4. Keep guest names and recurring terms in a [custom dictionary](/docs/guides/custom-dictionary/).
