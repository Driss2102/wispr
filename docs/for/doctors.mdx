# Wispr Flow for doctors

<DisclosureNotice />

**Doctors can use Wispr Flow to speed up the writing side of the job, emails, admin, referrals, and general notes, by dictating instead of typing. Two honest caveats are essential: it is general-purpose dictation, not a clinical tool like Dragon Medical with specialized medical vocabularies, and clinical or patient data needs the right compliance setup. Wispr Flow offers org-wide HIPAA enforcement on its Growth plan, but individual free or Pro use is not a compliance guarantee.**

This is not medical, legal, or compliance advice. Confirm your institution's rules and Wispr Flow's current terms before using it with any patient information.

## Where it helps

- **Administrative writing:** emails, referrals, letters, and internal notes.
- **General note-taking** that is not sensitive patient data.
- **Non-native English speakers** in medicine, writing cleanly in a second language. See [for non-native speakers](/docs/for/non-native-english-speakers/).

## The compliance reality

- **HIPAA:** available as org-wide enforcement on the [Growth plan](/docs/review/wispr-flow-pricing/), not on individual free or Pro plans. If you handle PHI, that distinction matters; check the current terms and your organization's approval.
- **Cloud by default:** speech is processed in the cloud unless configured otherwise, so treat patient data carefully. See [is Wispr Flow safe](/docs/review/is-wispr-flow-safe/).
- **Not a clinical dictation product:** Dragon Medical and similar tools ship deep medical vocabularies and clinical workflows. Wispr Flow relies on a [custom dictionary](/docs/guides/custom-dictionary/) for specialized terms.

## A safe way to start

Use it first for non-clinical admin and correspondence. Only bring it near patient data if your organization has approved it on a HIPAA-enabled plan.
