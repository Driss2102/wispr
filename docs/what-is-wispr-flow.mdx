# What is Wispr Flow?

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow is an AI dictation app made by Wispr AI, Inc. You hold a keyboard shortcut, speak normally, and it types cleaned-up text wherever your cursor is: your email, a document, a chat, or a code editor. It removes filler words, adds punctuation, and works in 100+ languages on Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android.**

Think of it as voice typing that actually keeps up with how people talk. Instead of the stilted "period, new paragraph" of older dictation, you ramble, pause, and correct yourself, and Wispr Flow turns that into text that reads like you wrote it.

## What Wispr Flow does

- **Types in almost any app.** It works on top of Gmail, Slack, Notion, iMessage, the terminal, and AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Cursor, anywhere you can put a cursor in a text field.
- **Cleans up as you speak.** Filler words ("um", "you know") are dropped and punctuation is added automatically.
- **Adapts tone.** It can match a casual Slack message or a more formal email.
- **Learns your words.** A personal dictionary keeps names, jargon, and acronyms spelled the way you want.
- **Handles 100+ languages** with automatic language detection.

Wispr Flow says dictation this way is about 4x faster than typing (around 220 words per minute versus 45). That is the company's own claim, not our measurement, but it matches the general finding that most people speak far faster than they type. For the complete list, see [all Wispr Flow features](/docs/guides/wispr-flow-features/).

## Is Wispr Flow free?

Yes, there is a permanent free plan. As of 2026 it includes 2,000 words per week on desktop (1,000 on iPhone, unlimited on Android) with no credit card. When you outgrow the weekly cap, the paid Pro plan removes it. We cover this in detail on the [free plan page](/docs/review/wispr-flow-free-plan/) and the [pricing guide](/docs/review/wispr-flow-pricing/).

## Which devices does it run on?

Wispr Flow runs on **Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android**. There is **no native Linux app** as of 2026, which is a real gap if Linux is your main machine. See our [Linux page](/docs/use/linux/) for the honest workarounds.

## Wispr Flow vs "Whisper Flow" vs OpenAI Whisper

This trips people up, so here it is plainly:

- **Wispr Flow** is the app on this site, made by Wispr AI.
- **"Whisper Flow"** is just a common misspelling of the same app (the brand is *Wispr*, no "h").
- **OpenAI Whisper** is a separate open-source speech-recognition *model*. It powers many transcription tools, but it is not an app you dictate with, and it is not Wispr Flow.

We untangle all three on the [Whisper Flow vs Wispr Flow](/docs/guides/whisper-flow-vs-wispr-flow/) page.

## Who it is for (and who it is not)

Wispr Flow fits you if you write a lot in short bursts across many apps: [solopreneurs](/docs/for/solopreneurs/), developers, writers, support agents, and anyone whose wrists are tired of the keyboard. It fits less well if you need offline, on-device-only dictation (it processes in the cloud by default), or if you are on Linux. See the full breakdown of [who uses Wispr Flow](/docs/for/who-uses-wispr-flow/).
