# Download Wispr Flow

<DisclosureNotice />

**Download Wispr Flow from its official source for your device: the Mac and Windows apps from wisprflow.ai, the iPhone app from the App Store, and the Android app from Google Play. Always download from the official site or the app stores, never a third-party mirror. There is no Linux download, because there is no native Linux app.**

## Where to download, by device

| Device | Where to get it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Mac** | Official site (wisprflow.ai) | Grant Microphone and Accessibility permissions after install. See [Mac setup](/docs/use/mac/). |
| **Windows** | Official site (wisprflow.ai) | Allow microphone access. See [Windows setup](/docs/use/windows/). |
| **iPhone** | App Store | Enable the Wispr Flow keyboard with Full Access. See [iPhone setup](/docs/use/iphone/). |
| **Android** | Google Play | Free plan is unlimited here. See [Android setup](/docs/use/android/). |
| **Linux** | Not available | No native app. See the [Linux options](/docs/use/linux/). |

## Download safely

- **Use official sources only.** The wisprflow.ai site and the two app stores. Voice apps need deep permissions, so a fake installer is exactly what you do not want.
- **Check the URL** is wisprflow.ai before downloading the desktop app.
- **Grant only the permissions asked** (microphone, and Accessibility on Mac so it can type into other apps).

## After you download

1. Install and open Wispr Flow, then sign in or create an account (see the [login guide](/docs/wispr-flow-login/)).
2. Grant microphone (and Accessibility on Mac) permissions.
3. Learn your dictation hotkey and test it in any text field.

You do not need to pay to start: the [free plan](/docs/review/wispr-flow-free-plan/) works after download with no credit card.
