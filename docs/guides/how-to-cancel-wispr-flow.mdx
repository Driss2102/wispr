# How to cancel Wispr Flow

<DisclosureNotice />

**You cancel a Wispr Flow subscription from your account or billing settings (in the app, or via the platform you subscribed through, such as the App Store on iPhone). Cancelling stops future billing and, at the end of your paid period, drops you to the free plan rather than deleting your account, so you keep using Wispr Flow with the free weekly word cap.**

We include this because honest guides cover leaving, not just joining. Knowing you can downgrade easily makes it lower-risk to try Pro.

## Where to cancel

- **Subscribed on desktop or the web:** open your account or billing settings in Wispr Flow and cancel there.
- **Subscribed on iPhone via the App Store:** cancel in iOS **Settings > your name > Subscriptions**, because Apple manages that billing.
- **Subscribed on Android via Google Play:** cancel in the **Play Store > Subscriptions**.

Always cancel where you originally subscribed; that is the platform charging you.

## What happens when you cancel

- **Billing stops** at the end of the current period.
- **You keep access** until that period ends.
- **You drop to the free plan**, not a deleted account, so you can keep dictating within the free weekly cap.
- **Your settings and dictionary** generally remain tied to your account.

## Consider downgrading instead of leaving

If Pro is more than you need but you still dictate sometimes, cancelling simply returns you to the [free plan](/docs/review/wispr-flow-free-plan/), which is unlimited on Android. You do not have to abandon the app to stop paying.

## Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on Wispr Flow's policy and, for mobile, the App Store or Google Play refund rules. This independent guide cannot issue refunds; request one through Wispr Flow support or the store you paid through.
