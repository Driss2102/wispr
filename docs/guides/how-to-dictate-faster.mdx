# How to dictate faster

<DisclosureNotice />

**You dictate faster not by talking faster, but by removing the things that slow you down: corrections, formatting fiddling, and using voice on the wrong tasks. Dial in your microphone and dictionary so you correct less, batch your writing, lean on snippets for repeated text, and reserve the keyboard for the precise bits. Do those, and your real throughput climbs well beyond your typing speed.**

## 1. Cut corrections at the source

Every correction is lost time. Reduce them by using a [good microphone](/docs/guides/best-dictation-microphone/) in a quiet room and adding your common words to a [custom dictionary](/docs/guides/custom-dictionary/). Fewer errors means fewer stops.

## 2. Do not narrate punctuation

Let Wispr Flow add punctuation from your phrasing instead of saying "comma, period" everywhere. Over-narrating is slower and can hurt accuracy. See [punctuation and formatting](/docs/guides/dictate-punctuation-formatting/).

## 3. Batch and separate stages

Draft everything in one dictation pass without stopping to fix, then edit in a second pass. Switching between talking and fixing constantly kills your rhythm; batching keeps you flowing.

## 4. Use snippets for repeats

Anything you write more than twice, a signature, a standard reply, a link, should be a [snippet](/docs/guides/wispr-flow-snippets/), not something you dictate each time.

## 5. Give voice the right tasks

Dictate prose (email, chat, docs, prompts). Type the precise bits (code, exact numbers, tiny edits). Fighting voice on tasks it is bad at is the biggest hidden time sink.

## 6. Learn one comfortable hotkey

A [hotkey](/docs/guides/keyboard-shortcuts/) you can hold without reaching means you dictate a sentence and get back to typing instantly, no friction, no hunting.
