# Whisper Flow vs Wispr Flow

<DisclosureNotice />

**"Whisper Flow" is almost always a misspelling of Wispr Flow, the AI dictation app made by Wispr AI. The brand is spelled Wispr, with no "h". Separately, OpenAI Whisper is an unrelated open-source speech model. So there are really two things people mix up: a spelling (Whisper Flow = Wispr Flow) and a different product (OpenAI Whisper). If you came here typing "Whisper Flow", the app you want is Wispr Flow.**

## The three names, sorted out

- **Wispr Flow**: the app. Made by Wispr AI, Inc. You install it, hold a hotkey, and it types what you say in any app. This whole site is about it.
- **Whisper Flow**: not a separate product. It is how people commonly (mis)type Wispr Flow, partly because "Wispr" looks like a typo and partly because of the next item.
- **OpenAI Whisper**: a separate, open-source speech-recognition *model* from OpenAI. Developers use it to build transcription and dictation tools. It is not an app you dictate with, and it is not made by Wispr AI.

## Why the confusion happens

The word "whisper" is a real word; "Wispr" is a stylized brand. On top of that, OpenAI's Whisper model is genuinely famous in the speech world, and some dictation apps are built on it. So "whisper" gets attached to anything voice-related. None of that changes the fact that the app is spelled Wispr Flow.

## Which one do you actually want?

- **You want to dictate text today, no coding:** you want **Wispr Flow**. Read [what it is](/docs/what-is-wispr-flow/) or the [review](/docs/review/).
- **You are a developer building your own tool, or need offline/Linux:** you may want **OpenAI Whisper** as a component. See [Wispr Flow vs OpenAI Whisper](/docs/compare/wispr-flow-vs-openai-whisper/).
