# How to use Wispr Flow

<DisclosureNotice />

**The core loop is simple: put your cursor in any text field, hold your dictation hotkey, speak naturally, and release. Wispr Flow cleans up filler and punctuation and types the result. The skill is not in the setup, it is in building the habit and learning to speak in a way that gets clean output the first time.**

## The basic loop

1. Click into a text field (email, chat, doc, editor).
2. Hold the dictation hotkey.
3. Speak in full thoughts. Pause when you think; the app handles pauses.
4. Release. The cleaned-up text appears.

## Speak for best results

- **Talk in sentences, not keywords.** The cleanup works better on natural speech than on staccato phrases.
- **Do not narrate punctuation** unless you need a specific layout. Wispr Flow adds it for you.
- **Correct out loud sparingly.** For small fixes, the keyboard is faster than re-dictating.

## Build the habit

Pick one recurring task and always dictate it: your first email, your standup update, or your AI prompts. Once one task is muscle memory, add another. Trying to switch everything at once is why most people abandon dictation.
