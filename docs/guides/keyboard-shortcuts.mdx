# Wispr Flow keyboard shortcuts and hotkeys

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow is driven by one main hotkey: hold it to dictate, release to stop. The key to a smooth setup is choosing a hotkey that is easy to hold and does not clash with shortcuts in the apps you use most. Get that right and dictation becomes something you reach for without thinking.**

## The main dictation hotkey

You hold the dictation hotkey while speaking and release when done. Because you hold it (rather than toggling), it is easy to fire off a quick sentence and get straight back to typing.

## Choosing a good hotkey

- **Pick something reachable with one hand** so you can dictate while your other hand is on the mouse.
- **Avoid conflicts.** If the default clashes with a shortcut in your editor, browser, or chat app, change it in Wispr Flow's settings.
- **Keep it consistent across machines** so the habit transfers.

## Keep dictation in your flow

The goal is to switch between typing and talking without friction. A well-placed hotkey means you dictate the sentence, release, and keep typing the precise bits. If you find yourself avoiding dictation, the hotkey is usually the reason; change it.
