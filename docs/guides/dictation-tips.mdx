# Dictation tips that actually help

<DisclosureNotice />

**The biggest accuracy gains come from three things: speaking in natural full sentences, reducing background noise, and teaching the app your specific words. Most "bad accuracy" complaints trace back to one of these, not to the app being broken.**

## Speak in full thoughts

Modern dictation is trained on natural speech, so complete sentences transcribe better than clipped keywords. Say the whole thought, pause to think if you need to, and let the cleanup handle the rest. Do not over-narrate punctuation.

## Fix your environment

- **Reduce background noise.** A quieter room beats any setting.
- **Use a decent microphone.** A headset mic close to your mouth is more accurate than a far-field laptop mic.
- **Keep a steady distance and volume.** Trailing off at the end of sentences causes errors.

## Teach it your words

Names, jargon, and acronyms are what general models get wrong. A short [custom dictionary](/docs/guides/custom-dictionary/) fixes the words you correct most, which is the highest-leverage tweak you can make.

## Match the tool to the task

Dictate prose (email, chat, docs, prompts). Type the precise bits (code, exact figures, tiny edits). Fighting the tool on tasks it is bad at is what makes people give up.
