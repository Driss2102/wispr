# How to dictate punctuation and formatting

<DisclosureNotice />

**With Wispr Flow you usually do not narrate punctuation, it adds commas, periods, and question marks automatically from the way you speak. You only say punctuation and formatting explicitly when you need a specific structure the app cannot infer, like a deliberate line break, a numbered list, or a particular layout. The rule of thumb: speak naturally first, and only add spoken formatting when the default is not what you want.**

## Let it punctuate for you

Modern dictation infers punctuation from your rhythm and phrasing. If you speak in natural sentences and pause where a sentence ends, Wispr Flow will place periods and commas for you. Over-narrating ("comma", "period") on top of that can actually make the output worse.

## When to say it explicitly

Say formatting out loud when you want structure the app would not guess:

- **New line or new paragraph** when you want a deliberate break.
- **Lists** when you want numbered or bulleted items.
- **Specific punctuation** in edge cases (for example, a colon before a list).

For the exact spoken commands your version supports, check Wispr Flow's [help center](https://docs.wisprflow.ai/), since the phrasing can change, and consider [Command Mode](/docs/guides/command-mode/) for reformatting after the fact.

## Formatting after you speak

Often the cleanest workflow is to dictate the content naturally, then use [Command Mode](/docs/guides/command-mode/) or the keyboard to format it, turning a paragraph into bullets, for example. Separating "get the words down" from "format it" is faster than doing both at once.
