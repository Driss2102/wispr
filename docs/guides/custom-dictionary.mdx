# Set up a Wispr Flow custom dictionary

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow's custom dictionary teaches it the words it keeps getting wrong: people's names, product and company names, technical jargon, and acronyms. Adding a dozen of your most-used terms is the single fastest way to improve accuracy, because these are exactly the words a general model has never seen.**

## Why it matters

General speech models are trained on common language, so they nail everyday words and stumble on the specific ones: your colleagues' names, your company's product, the libraries you use. Every time you fix the same word, you are doing work the dictionary should do once.

## What to add first

1. **Names** of people you write to often.
2. **Your company and product names**, and clients' names.
3. **Technical or industry jargon** you use daily.
4. **Acronyms** that should stay capitalized.
5. **Anything you have corrected more than twice.**

## How to keep it useful

- Add terms as you catch mistakes, not in one big session.
- Include the spelling and, where the app allows, how it sounds.
- Review it occasionally and remove terms you no longer use.

This is especially valuable for [developers](/docs/for/developers/) dictating library names and for anyone in a specialized field.
