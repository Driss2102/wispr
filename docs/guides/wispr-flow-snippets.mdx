# Wispr Flow snippets

<DisclosureNotice />

**Snippets are saved pieces of text you can drop in quickly instead of dictating or typing them every time: your email signature, a mailing address, standard replies, a booking link, or any boilerplate. Combined with dictation, they let you speak the unique part of a message and insert the repeated part in one step, which is a big time-saver for anyone who sends similar messages all day.**

## What snippets are for

- **Signatures and contact blocks.**
- **Standard replies** you send often (support answers, scheduling, FAQs).
- **Links** you paste repeatedly (calendar, portfolio, docs).
- **Boilerplate** legal or disclaimer text.

## Snippets plus dictation

The pattern that saves the most time is hybrid: dictate the personal, one-off part of a message, then insert a snippet for the standard part. A support agent might dictate "Thanks Priya, that is fixed now" and drop in a signoff snippet, all without typing.

## Getting started with snippets

Start by turning your three most-repeated pieces of text into snippets: your signature, your most common reply, and your most-shared link. Add more as you notice yourself typing the same thing twice. For the exact setup steps in your version, see Wispr Flow's [help center](https://docs.wisprflow.ai/).

## Snippets vs the custom dictionary

They solve different problems. [Snippets](/docs/guides/wispr-flow-snippets/) insert whole blocks of saved text. The [custom dictionary](/docs/guides/custom-dictionary/) teaches Wispr Flow to spell specific words correctly when you dictate them. Use both.
