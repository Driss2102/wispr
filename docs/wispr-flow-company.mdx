# Wispr AI, the company behind Wispr Flow

<DisclosureNotice />

**Wispr Flow is made by Wispr AI, Inc., a San Francisco company founded in 2021 by Tanay Kothari (CEO) and Sahaj Garg. In August 2026 it raised a $280 million Series B led by Menlo Ventures at a $2 billion valuation, bringing its total funding to $361 million. That matters because a well-funded, fast-growing company is more likely to keep improving the app and stay around, which is worth knowing before you build a habit on it.**

## The basics

- **Company:** Wispr AI, Inc.
- **Founded:** 2021.
- **Founders:** Tanay Kothari (CEO) and Sahaj Garg.
- **Headquarters:** San Francisco.
- **Official website:** [wisprflow.ai](https://wisprflow.ai/).
- **Main product:** Wispr Flow, the AI dictation app this site reviews.

## Funding and valuation

According to reporting from TechCrunch and Fortune (August 2026), Wispr raised a **$280 million Series B led by Menlo Ventures at a $2 billion valuation**, up from roughly a $700 million valuation in November 2025. Total funding to date is about **$361 million**. Investors include Menlo Ventures (lead) alongside existing and new backers.

These figures are the reported round numbers, not our estimate. For the primary reporting, see [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/wispr-raises-280m-at-2b-valuation-as-it-looks-beyond-dictation/) and [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/17/wispr-2-billion-valuation-dictations-only-the-beginning/).

## Why the company matters to you

You are not just buying a feature; you are betting a workflow on a product that needs to keep existing and improving. Strong funding and rapid growth are reassuring signals here: they suggest the app will keep getting updates and is unlikely to disappear soon. They are not a guarantee, and they do not make the product right for everyone, but for a tool you use daily, the company's health is a fair thing to weigh.

The company has also signaled it is [expanding beyond dictation](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/wispr-raises-280m-at-2b-valuation-as-it-looks-beyond-dictation/) into meetings, via its Notetaker feature. Whether that focus helps or dilutes the core dictation experience is something we will keep watching.
