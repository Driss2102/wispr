# Wispr Flow pricing — summary by Wispr Flow Guide

> Independent summary from Wispr Flow Guide (not Wispr AI). Wispr Flow's prices
> vary by region and change over time, so exact figures are intentionally not
> quoted here. See https://wisprflow.ai/pricing for current prices, or our guide
> at https://wisprflowguide.com/docs/review/wispr-flow-pricing/ .

## Free
- Price: $0, no credit card required
- Word limit: 2,000 words per week on desktop, 1,000 per week on iPhone, unlimited on Android (as of 2026-09-13)
- Includes: dictation in 100+ languages across Mac, Windows, iOS and Android; personal dictionary and snippets; limited Notetaker
- Note: this is a permanent free plan (not a time-limited trial)
- Best for: light or occasional dictation, and testing the app before paying

## Pro
- Price: paid, billed monthly or annually; annual is about 20% cheaper per month. See https://wisprflow.ai/pricing for the current figure.
- Adds over Free: unlimited dictation, longer Notetaker history, priority support, early access to features
- Best for: daily drivers who hit the free weekly word cap

## Growth
- Price: paid, higher tier than Pro. See https://wisprflow.ai/pricing for the current figure.
- Adds over Pro: SSO/SAML, org-wide HIPAA enforcement, admin controls, individual usage reporting
- Best for: teams and organizations needing security and admin controls

## Enterprise
- Price: custom, sales-led (annual only)
- Adds: free IT admin seats, locked model training, SCIM provisioning, audit logs, custom contracts, dedicated support with SLAs
- Contact: https://wisprflow.ai/

## Key facts (2026)
- Wispr Flow is AI voice dictation: you speak, it types cleaned-up text in almost any app (Gmail, Slack, Notion, ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, code editors, and more).
- It automatically removes filler words and adds punctuation, and adapts tone to the app.
- Supports 100+ languages with automatic language detection.
- Runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android. There is no native Linux app as of 2026-09-13.
- Wispr Flow says dictation is about 4x faster than typing (around 220 words per minute versus 45); this is the company's claim.
