# Affiliate Disclosure

Wispr Flow Guide is an independent website. We are not owned by, operated by, or officially affiliated with Wispr AI, Inc.

## How it works

- We include referral links to Wispr Flow throughout our reviews, setup guides, and buying advice.
- If you click a link and then sign up for a paid plan, we may earn a commission.
- The price you pay is the same whether you use our link or navigate directly.
- Attribution is tracked via cookies. Wispr Flow's program uses a 60-day cookie window.
- Each affiliate program has its own terms, commission rates, and payout conditions. Consult the official terms of each program for specifics.

## Our editorial independence

Affiliate relationships do not determine what we write or how we rate tools. Our editorial process is:

- **Honest:** we describe limitations and failure cases, not just upsides.
- **Balanced:** when a competitor is better suited for a task, we say so, including when that means steering away from a commission.
- **Source-verified:** pricing and feature claims are checked against official documentation, not taken from marketing copy.

We earn our primary affiliate revenue from Wispr Flow. This gives us a financial incentive to write thorough, accurate Wispr Flow content, not to inflate its capabilities or hide its weaknesses. Misleading guides drive away the readers this site depends on.

## Comparison and "vs" pages

Our comparison and "alternatives" pages are written to help you choose, not to sell. They do not carry affiliate links to keep them neutral; where we recommend Wispr Flow, we link you to our review, which does contain an affiliate link.

## What your support funds

Commission revenue from affiliate links goes toward hosting, the time spent researching and testing, keeping all content free, and writing new guides as the tools change.

## Sponsored content

We currently have no sponsors. If an article is ever a sponsored placement or a guest post, it will be clearly labeled at the top of that article and marked with `rel="sponsored"` links. Sponsors do not control our editorial coverage of their products elsewhere on this site.
